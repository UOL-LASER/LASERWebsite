Liverpool Association For Space Engineering And Research

A University of Liverpool student-run engineering society based in the Electrical and Electronics Engineering department.

01 / LASER

Design, Build, Learn.

LASER is a student-run engineering society where people from every background can work together on ambitious projects, learn practical skills and make friends along the way.

02 / OUR SPACE

Our private lab space

Our private lab is a place to study, collaborate and turn ideas into working hardware. It includes dedicated study space, 3D printers for rapid prototyping and a soldering station for electronics work.

03 / COMPETITIONS

Our rocketry team

We compete in the UKSEDS National Rocketry Championship and Exotopic Mach-X Competition. In 2026, we won the UKSEDS National Rocketry Championship with additional awards for "Best Rideshare Payload" and the "Public Engagement Award".

FAQ:

Some common questions commonly asked about LASER from prospective members.

How can I start a project?

To start a project just come along and bring it up in a meeting.

Does it cost anything?

No, participation in LASER does not cost anything apart from your time.

How do I join?

As we're not affiliated with the Guild, we have no sign up requirements. Simply just show up to our meetings as listed below.

What sort of things do you do?

Within LASER we mainly work to develop skills outside of the typically taught curriculum through projects, in addition we offer a range of FYP for third year EEE students.

What competitions do you take part in?

We take part in a range of student engineering and space competitions, including rocketry, rover and cubesats.

I lack technical skills/knowledge, can I still take part?

We encourage anyone regardless of course and background with an interest to join, as our projects often cover a wide range of fields.

Terms of Service

Last updated: December 2024

These Terms of Service (ToS) outline the rules, responsibilities, and processes for all members and committee members of LASER within the University of Liverpool. By participating in the society, You agree to these Terms.

General Conduct

  • Respect and Inclusion: All individuals must treat others with respect, regardless of background, opinion, or role within the society. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited.
  • Professionalism: Members and committee members must act professionally, particularly when discussing sensitive matters like finances or personal disputes.
  • Conflict Resolution: Disagreements must be handled respectfully and in line with society processes.

Financial Conduct

  • Transparency: All financial transactions must be documented clearly and openly, including deposits, event funds, and reimbursements.
  • Equality in Financial Access: Members may not personally benefit from society funds without approval from the Chief Financial Officer(s).
  • Documentation: Receipts or proof of transactions must be maintained on LASER SharePoint.
  • Confidentiality: Personal financial details must be handled securely and only by authorized individuals.

Responsibilities of Members

  • Participation: Members are encouraged to actively participate in society events.
  • Reporting Issues: Members must promptly report concerns like misconduct or financial irregularities.
  • Policy Adherence: Members must follow society policies, including reporting and disciplinary procedures.

Responsibilities of Committee Members

  • Leadership: Committee members must act as role models and uphold the society’s values.
  • Decision-Making: Decisions must prioritize the society's best interest, free from personal bias.
  • Accountability: Committee members are accountable for their decisions and actions.

Disciplinary Actions

Failure to adhere to these ToS may result in disciplinary actions, including:

  • Formal Warnings: Up to three warnings for rule violations.
  • Suspension or Expulsion: For serious or repeated violations.
  • Escalation: Serious incidents may be escalated to the University of Liverpool.

Financial Dispute Resolution

To resolve financial disputes:

  • Members must submit concerns in writing to the Society President.
  • The President and Chief Financial Officer(s) will review and respond.
  • Unresolved issues may escalate to a formal committee meeting.

Reporting Violations

  • Reports must include evidence where possible (e.g., screenshots, witness statements).
  • Anonymous reports will be accepted, but anonymity may limit investigation scope.
  • All reports are handled confidentially with outcomes communicated promptly.

Academic Integrity and Original Work

  • Original Contributions: All work submitted for LASER projects must be original.
  • Plagiarism: Plagiarism is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

Amendments to the Terms of Service

These Terms may be updated periodically. Members will be notified of changes promptly.
